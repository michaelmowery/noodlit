Need help or have an idea? Email mountmtech@gmail.com. Tell us your device and the scene you were using, and we'll get back to you.

What is Noodlit?

A scratchpad for getting thoughts out of your head. Draw or type on a scene, and it clears itself: steam fogs over the shower door, a letter gets mailed, your lines lift off as fireflies. Nothing is saved, so there's nothing to tidy up.

How do I clear the screen?

Fade scenes clear on their own. Use the speed button to make them fade faster or slower, or Hold to pause. Every scene also has a clear button in the toolbar (like Steam up or Mail it) that clears it right away.

How do I change scenes?

Tap the scene name at the top right to choose from all the scenes, grouped by mode: Fade, Release, Become, Reveal, Stir and Echo.

How do I type instead of draw?

Tap the keyboard button in the toolbar. Tap the surface to place your words, drag to move them, and tap Done to let them fade.

Not sure how a scene works?

Tap Show me at the top right for a short demo. About → Show me everything takes you on a tour of the whole app.

Can I get a drawing back?

No. Noodlit never saves what you draw or type, and nothing leaves your device. That's the point: let it go.

Sound

Use the speaker button at the top right to turn scene sounds on or off.

Privacy

Noodlit has no accounts and keeps your drawings on your device. It sends anonymous usage counts that you can turn off in Preferences. Read the privacy policy.
