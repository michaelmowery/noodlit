Get it out. Let it go. Just Noodlit.

A fading scratchpad for getting ideas out of your head.

Noodlit is a place to think out loud. Scribble, sketch, or type whatever is rattling around, and let it go. There is nothing to save, sort, or judge, so the next idea always has room.

It started on a foggy shower door, where the good ideas seem to show up. You write one in the steam, and the fog quietly takes it back.

Fade

Disappears on its own. Your marks soften and vanish at the pace you pick. Hold freezes a thought while you look at it.

Release

Stays until you let it go. Write it down, then send it off down a stream, in a bottle, or up with a lantern.

Become

Turns into something. Pause, and your lines wake up as fireflies, stars, flowers or meltwater, then slip away.

Reveal

Uncovers something. Scratch off foil to find a prompt, scrape through black to the colours below, or wipe a misty window to see out.

Stir

Swirls together. Milk in coffee, paint in rinse water or light in a kaleidoscope: your lines wind into each other until they mix away.

Echo

Plays it back. Pause, and your lines are redrawn stroke by stroke by a canyon's echo, a neon sign or a skywriting plane, then let go.

Early preview

Noodlit is brand new and growing fast. New scenes land often, and now and then something may wobble. Ideas, bugs, or a scene you'd love to see? We'd like to hear it.

Scenes in the Plus section of the scene menu will be part of Noodlit Plus when Noodlit arrives in the app stores. While we're in preview, every scene is free.

Privacy

Your drawings and words never leave your device. Nothing is saved or uploaded, and there's no account.

To learn which scenes and features people use, Noodlit sends anonymous counts (like "Snow was opened") to GoatCounter. No cookies, no ads, and nothing that follows you around the web. You can turn it off in Preferences.
