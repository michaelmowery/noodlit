Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Noodlit is made by Mount M Technologies, LLC. It's a scratchpad built for letting go, so it's built to know as little about you as possible.

Your drawings and words stay on your device

Everything you draw or type in Noodlit stays on your device and is never uploaded, saved to a server, or seen by us. There are no accounts.

Settings saved on your device

Noodlit remembers things like your last scene, fade speed, brush and preferences in your browser's local storage on your device. They never leave it, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which scenes and features people use, Noodlit sends anonymous counts to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. A count looks like "the Snow scene was opened" or "Show me was started". Each one includes:

  • the name of the action or page,
  • the page that linked you here, if any,
  • your screen size, and your browser and operating system (from the standard browser header),
  • an approximate country or region, worked out from your IP address. GoatCounter doesn't store the IP address itself.

GoatCounter uses no cookies and builds no profile of you. It tells repeat visits apart with a short-lived hash that is regularly thrown away, so it can't follow you over time or across other websites. The counts are never sold, never used for advertising, and never combined with other data. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

You can switch this off at any time in Noodlit under About → Preferences → Anonymous usage counts. Nothing is sent while you're offline.

Children

Noodlit doesn't ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.
